Travel

All business travel must be booked through the Northwind Travel Desk at least fourteen calendar days before departure. Bookings made inside the fourteen-day window require written approval from a department head, and the approval must be attached to the trip record before tickets are issued.

Approved carriers

Employees travelling within the European Union should use rail where the journey is under four hours door to door. Flights within that range are approved only when the total cost, including transfers, is at least thirty percent lower than the equivalent rail fare.

Accommodation

The nightly accommodation ceiling is 180 EUR in Berlin, Munich and Hamburg, and 140 EUR elsewhere in Germany. Stays above the ceiling need pre-approval and a short written justification.

Expenses

Expense claims are submitted in the Finance portal within thirty days of the expense being incurred. Claims older than ninety days are not reimbursed except in cases of extended illness or parental leave.

Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop, a docking station, and a headset. Hardware is replaced on a three-year cycle, or sooner if a fault cannot be repaired within five working days. Requests for a second monitor or a standing desk go through the Workplace team and do not require a manager's signature.

Personal devices may connect to the guest network only. Company data must not be stored on personal hardware, and the mobile device management profile is required on any phone that receives company mail.

Leave

The standard annual leave allowance is thirty days for full-time employees, accrued monthly. Up to ten unused days may be carried into the following year and must be taken before 31 March.

Sick leave requires a medical certificate from the fourth consecutive day. Parental leave follows the statutory scheme, with Northwind topping salary up to one hundred percent for the first twelve weeks.
